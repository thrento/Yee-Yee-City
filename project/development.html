About Us

Inspired by the ASMR youtuber RelaxingASMR Gaming, Yee Yee City was created similarly to his Minecraft realm 'patronville' where many patrons of his community came together to create their own creative Minecraft city.

Yee Yee City was created with this same idea in mind where members of our own community comes together to achieve those some goals of creativity and collaboration. Our community is dedicated to fostering a supportive and creative environment where everyone can contribute and thrive.

Join this realm to gain access to a vibrant community, share your creativity, gain inspiration, and be part of a collaborative and supportive environment.
